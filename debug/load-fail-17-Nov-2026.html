Indore to Pune Bus Timings and Duration

The first bus from Indore leaves at 00:45 and the last one at 23:56. A trip takes around 13 hrs 22 mins, though the exact time changes with traffic, the season and the operator.

Cancellation, FlexiTicket and Free Cancellation

FlexiTicket and Free Cancellation are two different things, and can be purchased separately. 

 

FlexiTicket lets passengers change the travel date for free up to 8 hours before departure, so there is no need to cancel and book again. Free Cancellation gives a full refund if the ticket is cancelled up to 6 hours before departure. 

 

Outside those windows, the operator's own cancellation charges apply. They are shown on the ticket at the time of booking.
